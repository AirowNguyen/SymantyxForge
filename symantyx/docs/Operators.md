# Operator Theory
Symantyx · Operator Layer

## Overview
Operators are functional units that transform packets, hypertuples, or semantic structures.

## Classes of Operators

### 1. Hydration Operators
Convert raw text → tokens → etymons → concepts → hypertuples.

### 2. Collapse Operators
Apply medium-specific collapse physics.

### 3. Binding Operators
Bind packets to membranes, geometry, and domain telos.

### 4. Emission Operators
Emit hypertuples into registry or canvas.

### 5. Spectral Operators
Analyze and propagate semantic frequency bands.

## Notes
Operators MUST remain pure functions.
Operators MUST be composable.
Operators MUST be membrane-safe.
